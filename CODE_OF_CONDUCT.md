# Code of conduct

codegrave follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, disagree about
facts and sources rather than about people, and keep discussion on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
accepted in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@ashrawan](https://github.com/ashrawan),
privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block
people who break these rules.
